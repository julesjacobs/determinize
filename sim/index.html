Mode
Whether a draw is kept random or replaced by its mean; affinity in the Lean development (Affinity).
E
Replaced by its mean in the determinized program.
G
Kept random; in one run, both programs draw the same value.
Sample site
A place in the program that draws from a distribution.
Symbolic state
The proof's symbolic semantics (paper, section 5): σ binds a symbol to each E draw so far, and the program computes with the symbols. The simulator computes it; it is not part of the Lean pipeline (its definitions in Lean).
G trace
The values of a run's G draws, in order (Trace).
Returned, rejected, failed
A run returns a value, is rejected by observe, or fails: division by zero, a parameter outside a distribution's domain, or the step limit.
Variance-reduction factor
The source's variance divided by the determinized program's, over returned runs.

The source pane holds your program, and the determinized pane what determinization makes of it. Draws marked E are replaced by their means. Draws marked G are kept random, and in each run both programs draw the same values for them. Step through one run below; under it, both programs run many times, and their outputs are compared.

Source

Write a program in the editor, or pick an example. A draw without a mark, such as uniform(0, 1), gets its mode from inference: E wherever that is allowed, which the determinized program replaces by its mean, and G, kept random, elsewhere. [G] or [E], as in gauss[E](x, 1), fixes a mode.
