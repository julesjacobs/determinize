\caption{Symbolic execution of the source program $e$ in~\Cref{fig:intro-signal}.
The $\G$-mode sample produces $t\sim\kw{uniform}(0,1)$ and the trace
$\tau_t$, whereas the two $\E$-mode samples are recorded in the symbolic
environment. Ordinary reductions (including \texttt{let} substitutions) are
omitted between states. The terminal state has two interpretations: the source
output distribution (left) and its mean (right). Both terminal interpretations
record only the empty \emph{remaining} trace $[]$, since the sample $t$ has
already been made.}
\label{fig:symbolic-example}
\Description{A vertical sequence of symbolic states starts from the lifted source
program, retains a uniform draw x=t, and records two Gaussian draws as dependent
symbolic variables u and v. The final residual expression is tv. Two arrows
branch to the actual interpretation, a Gaussian output with mean t squared and
variance 2t squared, and the expected interpretation, a point mass at t squared.}
