\caption{Implementation architecture. Both exact-computation routes establish
output moments for the selected program's measure $\law e$;
conditional mean and variance follow when return probability is positive.
Finite graph construction and the rational solver carry proofs of correctness.
Storm supplies exact vectors checked against the same model.
Portable certificates also allow independent checking of the graph.
The floating-point interpreter is unverified.}
\label{fig:architecture}
\Description{Compilation uses an unverified front end followed by verified typing
and determinization. Sampling and formal measure semantics are shown in parallel.
Verified finite graph construction and terminal reachability feed either a
verified rational solver or Storm followed by a verified vector checker.
Both routes produce proved probabilities and output moments.}
