\caption{Implementation architecture.}
\label{fig:architecture}
\Description{Compilation uses an unverified front end followed by verified type
checking, with a branch to the checked source and another through determinization.
One program branches to formal measure semantics, floating-point sampling,
and verified finite graph construction. The graph feeds either a
verified exact rational solver or Storm's exact model checking of expected
accumulated rewards, followed by a verified certificate checker.
Both routes produce exact mean and variance conditioned on returning, when return probability is positive.
A final panel lists the guarantees: determinization preserves the conditional
expectation, never increases the conditional variance, keeps the trace law and
replaces each conditional output law by the point mass at its mean, and the
variance removed equals the average within-trace variance.}
