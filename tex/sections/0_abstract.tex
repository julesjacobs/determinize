\begin{abstract}
We present \emph{determinization}, a type-directed, source-to-source transformation that automatically identifies and replaces eligible random samples by their means in higher-order, recursive probabilistic programs. Our type system tracks dependencies between random choices and restricts determinizable values to affine uses, enabling sound replacements without symbolic marginalization. We establish soundness through a symbolic operational semantics that relates the original and determinized programs, proving determinization preserves expected values and never increases output variance, and mechanize these guarantees in Lean. Beyond improving Monte Carlo estimation, determinization can eliminate continuous randomness entirely, transforming infinite-state programs into finite-state ones amenable to exact expectation computation. Our evaluation demonstrates that determinization enables exact analysis of programs beyond the supported languages of existing tools and reduces output variance in several sampling benchmarks drawn from prior literature and newly developed examples, with reductions exceeding two orders of magnitude. 
\end{abstract}
