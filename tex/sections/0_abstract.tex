\begin{abstract}
We present \emph{determinization}, a type-directed, source-to-source program transformation that automatically replaces eligible random samples by their means in higher-order, recursive probabilistic programs. Our type system tracks dependencies between random choices and restricts determinizable values to affine uses, enabling sound replacements without symbolic marginalization. We establish soundness through a symbolic operational semantics that relates the original and determinized programs, proving our transformation preserves expected values and never increases output variance. We mechanize these guarantees in Lean. By eliminating random samples, determinization can reduce a program's state space, such as transforming an infinite-state program into a finite-state one. Our evaluation shows that determinization enables computing exact expectations for programs combining continuous sampling with higher-order functions and recursion, and reduces output variance by over two orders of magnitude on several sampling benchmarks.
\end{abstract}
