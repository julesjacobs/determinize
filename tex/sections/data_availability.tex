\section*{Data-Availability Statement}

\ifleananonymous

  \fi
\else
  The artifact is available at \artifactlink.

It contains the Lean formalization, the command-line tool, the benchmarks and scripts of \cref{sec:evaluation}, and the test suites. Running \texttt{lake build -{}-wfail} in \texttt{lean/} checks every proof with Lean~4.34.1 and Mathlib, and fails unless every theorem in \texttt{Theorems.lean} depends only on the axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}; with Mathlib's prebuilt cache this takes about N minutes. The exact results of \cref{sec:evaluation} also need the Storm model checker (stormpy~1.14.0); the timings depend on the machine.
